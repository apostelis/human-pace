---
description: Open Human Pace settings with a live formatting preview and Save
allowed-tools: Bash(python3:*)
---
Run `python3 "${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/scripts/preview.py" --settings --open` with Bash. This opens a local settings page with live preview and Save. Give the returned URL if the browser does not open. Saved command-mode choices apply from the next ordinary prompt; saved native options may require a plugin reload or session restart. Do not apply human-pace formatting to this response.
